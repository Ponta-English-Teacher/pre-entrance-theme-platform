# Theme 4 Vocabulary Plan
## Living Together

Status: **Foundation, Standard, and Challenge finalized — Theme 4 vocabulary is structurally complete.** As with Themes 2 and 3, "structurally complete" means the word lists are decided; full entries (part of speech, CEFR tag, Japanese meaning, example sentence, etc. per `docs/VOCABULARY_ARCHITECTURE.md` §7) are not yet written.

Lexical sets: Relationships · Community & Belonging · Kindness & Respect · Agreement & Conflict · Diversity & Difference.

---

## Foundation Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (10 words)

**Note on count**: the original proposal had 11 words, including *community*. Moving *community* to Standard (see below) naturally settled this at 10 — accepted as-is. The vocabulary count is a guideline, not a target; educational rationale takes priority over numerical symmetry (see `docs/VOCABULARY_ARCHITECTURE.md` §5).

**Relationships**
- relationship
- trust
- close

**Community & Belonging**
- neighbor

**Kindness & Respect**
- kind
- respect

**Agreement & Conflict**
- agree
- disagree
- get along (with)
- share

**Reasoning**: Foundation gives students the language to describe their relationships and handle basic agreement/disagreement directly.

**Spiral reinforcement**: *kind* fulfills the reservation made in Theme 1, where it was cut for fitting this theme better. *share* was cut from Theme 1 for being too generic there, but becomes genuinely theme-specific here — students naturally talk about sharing ideas, responsibilities, feelings, and space when living together. *get along (with)* was a Theme 2 candidate not selected there — a far stronger fit here.

## Standard Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (8 words)

**Relationships**
- bond

**Community & Belonging**
- community
- belong

**Kindness & Respect**
- care

**Agreement & Conflict**
- cooperation / cooperate
- conflict

**Diversity & Difference**
- diverse / diversity
- understanding

**Reasoning**: Standard deepens students' ability to discuss relationships and community beyond simple description — naming connection (*bond*), group membership (*belong*), and more analytical registers of agreement/disagreement and difference.

**Spiral reinforcement, deliberately not downgraded**: *community* was introduced at Challenge level in Theme 2. It reinforces here at Standard — not Foundation — specifically to preserve the spiral principle that a word should deepen at its own level or higher, never regress to a lower one than its original introduction. *diverse/diversity* was also introduced at Challenge in Theme 2, similarly reinforced here at Standard as this theme engages with it more centrally.

## Challenge Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (5 words)

**Community & Belonging**
- society

**Kindness & Respect**
- considerate
- empathy

**Diversity & Difference**
- tolerance
- coexist

**Reasoning**: Challenge moves from participating in relationships to reflecting on them — the wider social unit (*society*), thoughtful behavior toward others (*considerate*), understanding others' inner experience (*empathy*), and the abstract work of accepting difference (*tolerance, coexist*). *Coexist* directly echoes this theme's own Purpose wording. *Empathy* carries strong spiral potential toward Theme 6 (Peace and War) and Theme 8 (Living with AI).
