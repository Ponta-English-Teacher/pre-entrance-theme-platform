# Theme 7 Reading & Writing Plan
## Caring for Our Planet — Foundation

Status: **Approved — design complete, not yet implemented in code.**

## 1. Reading Passage (180 words)

**Paragraph 1**
Aoi had been visiting the same beach with her family every summer
since she was five years old. This year, she noticed something
different: less sand, more plastic bottles, and far fewer of the small
crabs she used to chase as a child.

**Paragraph 2**
She asked her uncle, who had lived near the beach his whole life,
what had happened. He explained that pollution from nearby towns was
affecting the local environment, and that the ecosystem — all the
plants and animals connected to this one small beach — was slowly
changing because of it. He also explained that a warmer climate was
affecting the whole coastline.

**Paragraph 3**
Aoi did not know how to fix something so big, but she decided to
start small. She began carrying a reusable bottle instead of buying
new plastic ones, and she joined a weekend group that collected trash
from the beach. She learned that even one species, like the crabs she
loved, depended on people using resources like water and plastic more
carefully. It was not a perfect solution, but it was something she
could actually do.

**Takeaway Sentence**: "It was not a perfect solution, but it was
something she could actually do."

## 2. Vocabulary coverage

All six frozen Foundation words present: environment (P2), species
(P3), ecosystem (P2), climate (P2), pollution (P2), resource (P3, as
"resources").

**Spiral note**: none reinforce an earlier theme — all six are first
introductions.

## 3. Notice Language (4 activities)

- Find today's target vocabulary.
- Notice useful expressions/collocations ("start small," "not a
  perfect solution").
- Notice the in-text definition technique — "ecosystem" explained via
  a dash-set-off phrase within the same sentence.
- Notice paragraph organization — observation (P1), explanation of
  cause (P2), personal action (P3).

## 4. Comprehension (3 tasks)

**Task 1 — Choose the Answer**
*Question*: According to the passage, why did Aoi notice a change at
the beach this year?
- A. She had never visited the beach before this year.
- B. She saw less sand, more plastic, and fewer crabs than before. ✅
- C. Her uncle told her the beach had closed permanently.

**Task 2 — Supporting Evidence**
*Prompt*: Which paragraph explains the reasons behind the changes Aoi
noticed at the beach?
- Paragraph 1 (observation, no explanation yet)
- Paragraph 2 (her uncle explains pollution and climate) ✅
- Paragraph 3 (Aoi's personal response and actions)

**Task 3 — Vocabulary in Context**
*Question*: In "people using resources like water and plastic more
carefully," what does "resources" mean?
- A. Money that a person saves in a bank.
- B. Natural materials, like water, that people use in daily life. ✅
- C. Special skills that a person has learned.

*Design note*: chosen for domain-specific contextual understanding
(the passage guides students to the environmental sense of
"resources," distinct from its more common financial/skills
associations) rather than forced dictionary ambiguity.

## 5. Writing Task

**Prompt**: "Aoi decided to start small by carrying a reusable bottle
and joining a beach cleanup. Is there a small environmental habit you
already have, or one you would like to start? What is it?"

**Length**: 2–3 sentences (minimum 2).

**Optional sentence starters**:
- "I already try to..."
- "One small thing I could do is..."
- "I noticed that..."

**Useful Vocabulary for This Theme**: environment, species, ecosystem,
climate, pollution, resource. These are the words students have
learned this theme. Use whichever ones naturally help you express your
ideas — you are not expected to use every word. The goal is natural
communication, not vocabulary coverage.

**Why this fits Foundation**: describes a personal habit or small
intention — no environmental policy debate or scientific analysis
required.

**Connection to the reading**: mirrors Aoi's notice → think → act
progression directly.

**Connection to AI Talk**: previews an opener — "Aoi started with one
small habit to help the environment. Is there one small thing you
already do, or would like to start doing?"
