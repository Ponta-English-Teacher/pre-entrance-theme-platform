# Theme 6 Vocabulary Plan
## Peace and War

Status: **Foundation, Standard, and Challenge finalized — Theme 6 vocabulary is structurally complete.** Built under the corrected Foundation definition in `docs/VOCABULARY_PHILOSOPHY.md` §3 (indispensable concepts, not easy vocabulary) — this is the first theme developed under that principle.

Lexical sets: Peace & Conflict · Cooperation & Resolution · History & Consequence · Understanding Others · Global Awareness.

This theme requires particular care in register, per `docs/CURRICULUM_MAP.md`: it stays in historical/educational reflection, not graphic or militaristic vocabulary.

---

## Foundation Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (8 words)

**Peace & Conflict**
- peace
- war

**Understanding Others**
- victim
- refugee
- civilian

**Cooperation & Resolution**
- treaty

**History & Consequence**
- history
- legacy

**Reasoning**: Foundation here means the indispensable concepts needed to understand this theme, not easy words — *victim, refugee, civilian, treaty* are sophisticated but necessary for real engagement with what conflict and peace actually mean, not just abstract discussion of them. *History* is introduced here (named directly in this theme's vocabulary areas) and will deepen in Theme 9. *Legacy* replaces a more basic "lesson" — a less familiar but more precise word for what remains from historical events.

**Explicitly excluded from Foundation, moved to Reading Only**: *hope* and *safe* — both already well known to this population; an earlier draft of this theme wrongly promoted them for thematic centrality alone, which the corrected Foundation definition specifically warns against.

## Challenge Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (9 words)

**Cooperation & Resolution**
- resolve / resolution
- negotiate / negotiation
- diplomacy
- reconciliation

**Understanding Others**
- empathy

**Global Awareness**
- objective / objectivity
- perspective
- bias / biased
- humanity

**Reasoning**: Challenge moves from participating in conflict resolution to reflecting on it — how conflicts genuinely end, how humans understand each other across the divide of conflict, and how historical accounts of it are judged fairly.

**Spiral reinforcement**: *empathy* fulfills the reservation explicitly made in Theme 4. *Objective/objectivity, perspective,* and *bias/biased* all reinforce Theme 5, each explicitly anticipated there as belonging to this theme.
