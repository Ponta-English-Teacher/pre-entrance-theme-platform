## Foundation Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (7 words)

**Lessons & Legacy**
- history
- legacy

**Historical Events**
- event
- era

**Cause & Effect**
- cause
- effect

**Past & Present**
- ancestor

**Reasoning**: *event, cause, effect* are named directly in this theme's vocabulary areas. *era* and *ancestor* are indispensable for discussing historical time and personal connection to it (essential question 3: "why does history matter to me personally").

**Spiral reinforcement**: *history* and *legacy* reinforce Theme 6 at the same level, now becoming this theme's own central focus.

**Considered and excluded**: *change* — named directly in this theme's vocabulary areas, but already well known (consistent with its Reading Only treatment in Themes 1 and 2); the concept is carried instead by *transformation* (Standard).

## Challenge Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (5 words)

**Lessons & Legacy**
- perspective
- objective / objectivity
- bias / biased
- humanity

**Past & Present**
- evolution
- inevitable / inevitability

**Reasoning**: Challenge asks whose history gets told and how — the theme's deepest reflective register.

**Spiral reinforcement**: *perspective, objective/objectivity,* and *bias/biased* reinforce Theme 5/Theme 6 at the same level — genuinely justified here, since history-writing is inherently about perspective and bias. *humanity* reinforces Theme 6 at the same level.

**Worth flagging for Theme 10**: this cluster (*perspective, objective, bias*) has now appeared in three consecutive themes (5, 6, 9) — well-earned each time, but getting well-worn; Theme 10 should be selective about reusing it again.
