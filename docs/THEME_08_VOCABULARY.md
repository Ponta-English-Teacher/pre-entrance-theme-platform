## Foundation Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (5 words)

**AI & Technology**
- artificial intelligence / AI
- technology
- automation
- algorithm

**Change & Future**
- convenience

**Reasoning**: All named directly in this theme's vocabulary areas or indispensable to it — *algorithm* is essential to actually understanding how AI works, not merely discussing it as an abstract topic.

**Considered and excluded**: *human* — the concept of "human vs. AI" is central to this theme, but the word itself is already well known; the concept is carried by the other target words instead, and *human* sits in Reading Only.
