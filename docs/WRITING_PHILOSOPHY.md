# Writing Philosophy

Status: **Frozen — the permanent educational philosophy for the Writing activity, across all ten themes and both levels.** Mirrors the relationship between `docs/VOCABULARY_PHILOSOPHY.md` and `docs/VOCABULARY_ARCHITECTURE.md`: this document governs *why* Writing tasks are shaped the way they are; `docs/READING_WRITING_ARCHITECTURE.md` governs the production blueprint (length, task tiers) that follows from it.

---

The purpose of the Writing activity is different from the purpose of the Reading activity.

Reading asks:

"Can the student understand the ideas in the passage?"

Writing asks:

"Can the student express meaningful ideas in English?"

Therefore, the Writing activity is NOT a comprehension test.

The Reading passage provides the topic, vocabulary, and ideas that inspire the student's thinking, but the Writing task should gradually shift the focus from the passage to the student.

The student's own opinions, experiences, goals, and reflections are the center of the Writing activity.

The passage serves as a starting point, not as the subject to summarize.

## Foundation

Students learn to express themselves clearly.

Typical tasks:

- opinions
- experiences
- reasons
- examples

The emphasis is on producing a coherent paragraph with simple but effective English.

## Advanced

Students deepen the quality of their expression rather than simply writing about a harder topic.

The writing prompt should encourage students to:

- reflect on themselves,
- apply ideas from the Reading activity to their own lives,
- compare ideas,
- explain reasons,
- organize multiple related ideas,
- express themselves using richer vocabulary and more mature paragraph organization.

Advanced Writing should not become another Reading comprehension exercise.

The increase in difficulty comes from richer language and more thoughtful expression, not from asking more difficult academic questions.

## Relationship among activities

Reading
→ introduces ideas.

Writing
→ personalizes those ideas.

AI Talk
→ expands those personal reflections through natural conversation.

Each activity has its own educational objective.

Reading should not test Writing.

Writing should not repeat Reading.

AI Talk should not tutor either activity.
