## 1. Reading Passage (196 words)

**Paragraph 1**
Ren had never been the kind of student with one clear dream. Ask him
what he wanted to become, and he would just shrug. Choosing a path
felt like an impossible decision — how could anyone know the right
answer this early? Some of his classmates already seemed so sure of
themselves, and that only made him feel further behind.

**Paragraph 2**
Then, one weekend, his little sister asked him to help her build a
small robot for a school project. Ren spent hours explaining each
step, and he realized he loved watching her understand something new.
It was a small moment, but it made him wonder if teaching others could
become part of his future.

**Paragraph 3**
That night, Ren made a small choice: he looked up what it would take
to teach or design things for a living. He did not have a full plan
yet, but for the first time, he had something that felt like a real
goal. It felt important to have even one small direction to move
toward, even if the rest of the path was still unclear. For now, that
was enough — a single step in a direction that finally felt like his
own.

**Takeaway Sentence**: "It felt important to have even one small
direction to move toward, even if the rest of the path was still
unclear."

## 2. Vocabulary coverage

All seven frozen Foundation words present: dream (P1), decide (P1, as
"decision"), step (P2, P3), choice (P3), plan (P3), goal (P3),
important (P3).

**Spiral reinforcement**: goal, dream, important reinforce Theme 1 at
the same level; decide reinforces Theme 2 at the same level.

## 4. Comprehension (3 tasks)

**Task 1 — Choose the Answer**
*Question*: According to the passage, why did Ren finally feel like he
had a goal?
- A. Ren found his goal after seeing that his classmates already knew
  what they wanted to do.
- B. A small moment of helping his sister made him think about his
  future differently. ✅
- C. His parents told him what he should study at university.

**Task 2 — Supporting Evidence**
*Prompt*: Which paragraph shows the specific event that first gave Ren
an idea about his future?
- Paragraph 1 (uncertainty, no specific event)
- Paragraph 2 (helping his sister build a robot) ✅
- Paragraph 3 (reflection and small choice)

**Task 3 — Vocabulary in Context**
*Question*: In "a single step in a direction that finally felt like
his own," what does "step" mean?
- A. Moving one foot in front of the other while walking.
- B. One small action or stage toward a bigger goal. ✅
- C. A stair you climb to go up to a higher floor.

## 5. Writing Task

**Prompt**: "Has a small moment ever made you think differently about
your future? If so, what happened? If not, describe a small
experience that might help you discover your future one day."

**Length**: 2–3 sentences (minimum 2).

**Optional sentence starters**:
- "One time, I..."
- "It made me think about..."
- "I haven't found it yet, but maybe..."

**Useful Vocabulary for This Theme**: goal, dream, decide, choice,
plan, step, important. These are the words students have learned this
theme. Use whichever ones naturally help you express your ideas — you
are not expected to use every word. The goal is natural communication,
not vocabulary coverage.

**Why this fits Foundation**: narrates a personal experience rather
than justifying an opinion — the "Answer" tier. The "if not" branch
ensures every student can respond, not only those with a ready answer.

**Connection to the reading**: mirrors Ren's arc — a small, ordinary
moment sparking a thought about the future.

**Connection to AI Talk**: previews an opener referencing Ren's story
directly.
