## Foundation Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (7 words)

**Goals & Dreams**
- goal
- dream

**Decision-Making**
- decide
- choice

**Planning & Achievement**
- plan
- step

**Values & Priorities**
- important

**Reasoning**: Foundation gives students the language to state a goal and explain a choice in simple, direct self-expression — the same communicative bar applied throughout this curriculum.

**Spiral reinforcement, not new introductions**: *goal, dream, important* are Theme 1 words, reappearing here in their most natural later home — a theme literally about designing one's future. *decide* is a Theme 2 word, reinforced here since "how my choices shape my future" is this theme's own essential question.

**Promoted from Reading Only**: *choice* was too basic to earn a slot in Theme 2, but has special importance here — it appears in this theme's own essential-question wording.

**Narrative echo, not literal reinforcement**: *step* was never a target word, but directly echoes Theme 1's own reading passage ("every small step brings you a little closer to your dream").

## Standard Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (9 words)

**Goals & Dreams**
- ambition

**Career & Work**
- career
- develop
- occupation

**Decision-Making**
- option
- path

**Planning & Achievement**
- achieve

**Values & Priorities**
- values
- passion

**Reasoning**: Standard extends students from stating a goal to reasoning about it — naming the drive behind a goal (*ambition*), the outcome of pursuing one (*achieve*), and the personal motivations behind a choice (*values, passion*).

**Spiral reinforcement**: *career* and *develop* are Theme 2 words, both explicitly anticipated there as belonging to this theme.

**A notable reversal, worth remembering as a working example**: *values* was cut entirely from Theme 1 Foundation for being too abstract to say naturally ("my values are..." isn't how a Foundation student talks). That same abstraction is exactly right at Standard, where students can discuss motivations analytically rather than only describe themselves directly.

## Challenge Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (7 words)

**Goals & Dreams**
- aspiration

**Career & Work**
- profession

**Decision-Making**
- consider

**Planning & Achievement**
- pursue

**Values & Priorities**
- priority
- meaningful
- purpose

**Reasoning**: Challenge moves from participating in the goal-setting process to reflecting on it — naming the concept of aspiration itself, weighing options analytically (*consider*), and asking what makes a path worth choosing at all (*priority, meaningful, purpose*).

**Revision from the original proposal**: *fulfillment* was replaced with *purpose*. *Purpose* is the more central concept for this theme and more likely to recur naturally across the curriculum — it connects not only to Theme 3 but plausibly to Theme 1 (self-understanding), Theme 4 (purpose within relationships/community), Theme 7 (purpose in environmental action), and Theme 8 (human purpose in an AI-shaped world), making it meaningfully stronger spiral vocabulary than *fulfillment* would have been.
