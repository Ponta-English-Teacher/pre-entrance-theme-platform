## Foundation Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (6 words)

**Scientific Thinking**
- science
- observe / observation
- hypothesis

**Everyday Phenomena**
- phenomenon
- experiment

**Curiosity & Discovery**
- curiosity

**Reasoning**: *science, phenomenon, experiment,* and *curiosity* are named directly in this theme's vocabulary areas. *observe/observation* and *hypothesis* are indispensable to genuine scientific thinking, not just discussing science as a subject — the difference between describing science and actually doing it.

---

## Standard Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (6 words)

**Evidence & Experimentation**
- evidence
- research
- data
- theory
- analyze / analysis

**Curiosity & Discovery**
- discover / discovery

**Reasoning**: *theory, analyze/analysis,* and *discover/discovery* deepen genuine scientific reasoning. *Theory* is deliberately distinct from Foundation's *hypothesis* — a hypothesis is a starting guess, a theory is well-supported — a distinction worth teaching explicitly, not redundancy.

**Spiral reinforcement**: *evidence* reinforces Theme 5 at the same level. *research* reinforces Theme 2 at the same level. *data* reinforces Theme 8 at the same level.

---

## Challenge Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (4 words)

**Scientific Thinking**
- objective / objectivity
- skeptical / skepticism

**Curiosity & Discovery**
- innovation
- empirical

**Reasoning**: Challenge reaches this theme's culminating concepts — the values underlying the scientific method itself.

**Spiral reinforcement**: *objective/objectivity* and *skeptical/skepticism* reinforce Theme 5 at the same level, completing their spiral arc as the scientific method's core values.

**Deliberately not reinforced here**: *perspective* — flagged in Theme 9 as a well-worn cluster (appearing in Themes 5, 6, and 9). It would fit ("scientific perspective vs. other ways of knowing"), but a fourth consecutive use felt like exactly the unnecessary repetition the self-review process is meant to catch. *objective* and *skeptical* complete the arc cleanly without it.
