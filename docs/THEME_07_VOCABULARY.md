# Theme 7 Vocabulary Plan
## Caring for Our Planet

Status: **Foundation, Standard, and Challenge finalized — Theme 7 vocabulary is structurally complete.** Built under the corrected Foundation definition in `docs/VOCABULARY_PHILOSOPHY.md` §3.

Lexical sets: Environment & Nature · Climate & Change · Resources & Consumption · Problems & Pollution · Action & Responsibility.

---

## Foundation Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (6 words)

**Environment & Nature**
- environment
- species
- ecosystem

**Climate & Change**
- climate

**Problems & Pollution**
- pollution

**Resources & Consumption**
- resource

**Reasoning**: All six are named directly in this theme's vocabulary areas or indispensable to discussing them meaningfully — *species* and *ecosystem* are sophisticated but necessary for any real engagement with environmental interdependence, not decorative additions.

## Standard Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (6 words)

**Resources & Consumption**
- consume / consumption
- waste
- renewable

**Action & Responsibility**
- responsibility

**Climate & Change**
- impact

**Sustainability**
- sustainability / sustainable

**Reasoning**: Standard deepens from naming the problem to discussing cause, practical action, and consequence.

**Spiral reinforcement**: *responsibility* reinforces Theme 2 at the same-or-higher level, now applied to environmental stewardship rather than personal independence.

---

## Challenge Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (6 words)

**Action & Responsibility**
- conserve / conservation

**Environment & Nature**
- biodiversity

**Climate & Change**
- climate change
- carbon footprint
- degrade / degradation

**Global Awareness**
- objective / objectivity

**Reasoning**: Challenge moves to the theme's largest-scale, most analytical concepts. *Climate* (Foundation) and *climate change* (Challenge) are deliberately distinct — the general concept versus the specific phenomenon, not redundant.

**Spiral reinforcement**: *objective/objectivity* reinforces Theme 5 at the same level, explicitly anticipated there as belonging to this theme.

**Considered and excluded**: *community* — a plausible reinforcement ("global community acting together"), but a weaker fit here than in Theme 4 or Theme 8; left out rather than forced.
