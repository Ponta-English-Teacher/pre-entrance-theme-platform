# Theme 2 Vocabulary Plan
## What Does It Mean to Be a University Student?

Status: **Foundation, Standard, and Challenge finalized — Theme 2 vocabulary is structurally complete.** "Structurally complete" means the word lists themselves are decided; full entries (part of speech, CEFR tag, Japanese meaning, example sentence, etc. per `docs/VOCABULARY_ARCHITECTURE.md` §7) are not yet written, so `docs/CONTENT_PROGRESS.md` still shows 🔶 In Progress rather than ✅ Complete for all three levels.

Foundation lexical sets: University Life · Academic Life · Independence & Responsibility · Expectations & Adjustment · Social Life.

Standard lexical sets: Academic Life · University Experience · Personal Growth · Transition · Future Goals — a deliberate shift from Foundation's "survive and adapt" framing toward "become an active university learner."

Challenge lexical sets: University as an Institution · Academic Thinking · Personal Development · Society & Community · Future Perspective — the shift from Standard's "participate successfully" toward "understand higher education, personal growth, and one's role in society," per the reasoning below.

- challenge
- improve

**11 words total.**

**Reasoning**: Foundation vocabulary helps students survive and adapt to university life. Standard shifts toward becoming an active university learner — the language needed to participate in academic life, reflect on personal growth, and begin thinking about future goals. These words were chosen not simply for difficulty, but because they significantly expand what students can discuss about university experience.

**Intentional spiral vocabulary** — expected to naturally reappear and deepen in later themes: *discussion, research, confidence, challenge, develop, career*.

**Worth noting for tracking accuracy**: *confidence* is not a first introduction — it's already a finalized Theme 1 Foundation word (as *confident*). Its appearance here is genuine spiral reinforcement in a new, more sophisticated context (per `docs/VOCABULARY_PHILOSOPHY.md` §5's two-question framework, its introduction theme is Theme 1, not Theme 2) — recorded here so it isn't miscounted as a *new* word toward Theme 2's Standard total. *Career* also strongly anticipates Theme 3 ("Designing My Future"), which looks like the natural place for it to deepen further.

**Not included in the finalized Standard list**: *dormitory/dorm, professor, degree, presentation, scholarship, freedom, budget, get along (with)* — resolved during Challenge review (see below); *degree* and *scholarship* became Challenge words, the rest became Reading Only.

**Also worth noting**: *lecture* was originally classified Reading Only in the candidate pool (as an already-familiar word). It's promoted to Standard target vocabulary here, moved out of the Reading Only list below — presumably because the *academic* sense (lecture-style learning vs. high school classes) earns its place even though the word itself is common.

## Challenge Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized

**University as an Institution**
- degree
- scholarship
- curriculum

**Academic Thinking**
- critical thinking
- thesis
- academic

**Personal Development**
- autonomy
- self-discipline

**Society & Community**
- community
- diversity

**Future Perspective**
- lifelong learning

**11 words total.**

**Reasoning**: Challenge is not simply harder vocabulary — its purpose is to help students think more deeply about the meaning of university education itself. Where Foundation is about adapting and Standard is about participating, Challenge is about understanding higher education, personal growth, and one's own role in society. These words are chosen to encourage discussion, reflection, and critical thinking, while also serving as spiral vocabulary for later themes — *community* and *diversity* in particular anticipate Theme 4 ("Living Together").

**Resolved from earlier candidate pools**: *degree* and *scholarship* were carried forward unclassified from the Standard review — both land here. *curriculum, critical thinking, thesis, academic, autonomy, self-discipline, community, diversity* all match the original Challenge candidate pool directly. *Lifelong learning* is a genuinely new addition, not previously proposed.

**Not selected, and now finalized as Reading Only** (since Challenge is the last level, nothing is left to carry forward): *seminar, tuition, uncertainty, overwhelmed* (dropped from the Challenge candidate pool without individual reasoning) and *dormitory/dorm, professor, presentation, freedom, budget, get along (with)* (the remaining words carried forward unresolved from Standard).
