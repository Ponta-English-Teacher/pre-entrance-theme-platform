## 1. Reading Passage (207 words)

**Paragraph 1**
From the outside, university can look a lot like high school —
students, classrooms, homework. From the very first day, though,
something feels different: students receive far fewer reminders, and
much more depends on their own decisions. Becoming a university
student means becoming more independent, with real responsibility for
your own time.

**Paragraph 2**
On her first day, Sora wasn't sure what to expect, and she felt both
excited and nervous walking across the busy campus. Her professors
would not tell her every step to take. She had to decide many things
for herself, one small choice at a time. Outside the library, a group
of students were inviting people to join their photography club. She
felt nervous, but she walked over anyway and introduced herself.

**Paragraph 3**
It took a few weeks for Sora to adjust to this new kind of freedom.
She had to manage her own schedule, and she made a few mistakes along
the way. By the time her first real assignment for her major was due,
she felt a little more sure about handling things on her own. She was
slowly learning what kind of student she wanted to become — someone
who could take responsibility for her own choices, even the ones that
felt uncertain at first.

**Takeaway Sentence** (for the `takeaway` field):

> "Becoming a university student means becoming more independent, with real responsibility for your own time."

  though" — a subtler contrast/qualification signal than "but" or
  "however."
- Notice the grammar pattern "By the time [X] was due, she felt
  [Y]" — connecting an event's timing to a change in feeling.

## 4. Comprehension (3 tasks)

**Task 1 — Choose the Answer**
*Question*: According to the passage, what is different about being a
university student compared to high school?
- A. University students receive the same amount of guidance as they
  did in high school.
- B. University students receive far fewer reminders and have more
  responsibility for their own time. ✅
- C. University students do not need to attend classes regularly.

**Task 2 — Supporting Evidence**
*Prompt*: Which paragraph shows the specific moment when Sora made an
independent decision on her own, without anyone telling her what to
do?
- Paragraph 1 (the general idea, not a specific moment)
- Paragraph 2 (Sora decides to join the photography club) ✅
- Paragraph 3 (several weeks of gradual adjustment)

**Task 3 — Vocabulary in Context**
*Question*: In "her first real assignment for her major," what does
"major" mean?
- A. A very important or serious thing.
- B. The main subject a student studies at university. ✅
- C. A type of military officer.

## 5. Writing Task

**Prompt**: "When you start university, what is one thing you would
like to do or learn? Why does it interest you?"

**Length**: 2–3 sentences (minimum 2).

**Optional sentence starters**:
- "When I start university, I want to..."
- "I feel a little nervous about..., but I'm excited to..."
- "One thing I want to do is..."

**Useful Vocabulary for This Theme**: campus, major, assignment,
independent, responsibility, manage, decide, expect, adjust, excited,
nervous, join, introduce. These are the words students have learned
this theme. Use whichever ones naturally help you express your ideas
— you are not expected to use every word. The goal is natural
communication, not vocabulary coverage.

**Why this fits Foundation**: states a personal intention, not a
justified opinion — matching the "Answer" tier.

**Connection to the reading**: mirrors Sora's decision to join a club
despite feeling nervous, using her story as the scaffold.

**Connection to AI Talk**: previews an opener referencing Sora's
choice directly, rehearsing the answer AI Talk will later ask the
student to elaborate on.
