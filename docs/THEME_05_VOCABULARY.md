# Theme 5 Vocabulary Plan
## Facts or Fake?

Status: **Foundation, Standard, and Challenge finalized — Theme 5 vocabulary is structurally complete.** As with Themes 2–4, "structurally complete" means the word lists are decided; full entries (part of speech, CEFR tag, Japanese meaning, example sentence, etc. per `docs/VOCABULARY_ARCHITECTURE.md` §7) are not yet written.

Lexical sets: Truth & Belief · Sources & Evidence · Reliability & Trust · Fact vs. Opinion · Deception & Bias.

This theme is about evaluating information — questioning claims, judging evidence, recognizing misinformation, and thinking critically before accepting what is read or heard — not about technology or "the internet" as a subject.

---

## Foundation Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (10 words)

**Truth & Belief**
- true
- fake
- believe
- doubt
- honest

**Sources & Evidence**
- source
- check

**Reliability & Trust**
- trust

**Fact vs. Opinion**
- fact
- opinion

**Reasoning**: Foundation gives students the language to state whether something seems true, question it, and name where it came from — the theme's most direct, communicative layer.

**Spiral reinforcement**: *honest* fulfills the reservation made in Theme 1, where it was cut for fitting this theme better — describing whether a source or person is truthful is exactly this theme's territory. *trust* reinforces Theme 4 at the same level, per the same-or-higher rule — reapplied here to sources and information rather than people.

## Challenge Vocabulary — ✅ Finalized (5 words)

**Reliability & Trust**
- objective / objectivity

**Deception & Bias**
- bias / biased
- perspective
- skeptical / skepticism
- manipulate / manipulation

**Reasoning**: Challenge moves from evaluating individual claims to reflecting on information itself — how perspective shapes presentation, how complete objectivity is difficult to achieve, and how information can be deliberately distorted.

**Revision from the original proposal**: *credible/credibility* was replaced with *objective/objectivity*. The theme's central purpose isn't only judging whether a source is credible, but recognizing that information is presented from different perspectives and that complete objectivity is hard to achieve — a better fit for this theme's deepest layer, and one with clear spiral potential toward Theme 6 (Peace and War), Theme 7 (Caring for Our Planet), and Theme 8 (Living with AI), all of which will return to the question of perspective and objectivity from different angles.
